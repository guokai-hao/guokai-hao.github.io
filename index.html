About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on reinforcement learning and intelligent decision-making in complex dynamic environments.

My current interests include efficient reinforcement learning, robust and safe decision-making, meta reinforcement learning, and multi-agent reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on generalization, adaptation, and coordination under uncertainty.

Research Interests

  • Efficient Reinforcement Learning: improving sample efficiency and policy optimization.
  • Robust & Safe Reinforcement Learning: reliable decision-making under uncertainty and safety constraints.
  • Meta Reinforcement Learning: fast adaptation and cross-task generalization.
  • Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning: cooperative decision-making and multi-agent coordination.
